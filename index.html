Stegosaurus

A famous plant-eater from the Late Jurassic period.

~9 mLength
~4 mHeight
~5 tEstimated mass

Stegosaurus walked on four sturdy legs and carried two rows of large plates along its back. Its spiked tail, often called a thagomizer, helped defend it from predators. It was a herbivore and lived in North America around 150 million years ago.

Measurements are paleontological estimates; the AR model is scaled approximately for comparison.
